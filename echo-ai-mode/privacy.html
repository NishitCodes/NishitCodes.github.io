Privacy Policy for Echo AI Mode

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Echo AI Mode ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you use our Amazon Alexa skill.

1. Information We Collect

Echo AI Mode does not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number).

  • Voice Input: When you speak to Echo AI Mode, Amazon Alexa transcribes your spoken question into text and transmits the text string to our skill service solely to generate an AI response. We do not store raw audio recordings.
  • Anonymous Identifiers: Amazon assigns an anonymous, obfuscated user ID for each device/account. We use this anonymous identifier solely to track your daily question count (up to 30 free questions per day) to prevent service abuse.

2. Third-Party AI Processing

To provide intelligent, conversational responses, user queries are processed using the Google Gemini API. Queries are transmitted securely and processed in accordance with Google's API data governance policies. We do not use your personal conversations to train machine learning models.

3. Data Retention & Security

Daily question counters reset automatically every 24 hours. Conversation context within an active session is held only in volatile memory during the active dialogue and is cleared when you say "stop", "exit", or when the session ends.

4. Children's Privacy

Echo AI Mode is a general-purpose knowledge and conversational assistant. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via GitHub at github.com/NishitCodes.
